It's a small script for Hammerspoon, a free Mac app. You don't need it: without it everything still works. Set-up steps are in the README, under "Optional: the Mac helper".

Privacy

Your key is stored only in this extension, on this Mac. It is never synced and only ever sent to api.anthropic.com. Websites and other extensions cannot read it.
